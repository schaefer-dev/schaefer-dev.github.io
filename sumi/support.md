# Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[sumi@schaefer-dev.de](mailto:sumi@schaefer-dev.de)**. Please mention your device, iOS version and the app version (shown in **Settings → About**). If a particular file does not open, say whether it opens on the Supernote itself; please only attach files you are comfortable sharing.

## Frequently asked questions

<div class="sumi-faq">

<details>
<summary>How do I get my notebooks into Sumi?</summary>
<p>Turn on Dropbox sync in your Supernote's sync settings. In Sumi, tap <strong>Connect Dropbox</strong>, sign in, and choose the folder your Supernote syncs to, usually <code>Supernote</code>. Your notebooks and annotated PDFs then appear under <strong>Browse Dropbox</strong>.</p>
<p>Without Dropbox, the start screen offers <strong>Open from Files…</strong> and a few sample notebooks to try. You can also always share a file to Sumi from another app, or open it from the Files app.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Sumi change or delete my files?</summary>
<p>No. Sumi asks Dropbox for read-only access. It can list and download your files, but it can never change, upload or delete anything.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which files are supported?</summary>
<p>Supernote notebooks (<code>.note</code>), annotation files (<code>.mark</code>) shown on their original PDF, and ordinary PDFs. Notebooks in the original first-generation Supernote file format are not supported.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>An annotated PDF shows only my annotations.</summary>
<p>A <code>.mark</code> file holds only your handwriting; the page content comes from the PDF next to it. From Dropbox, Sumi finds the original automatically when it sits in the same folder with the matching name (<code>Book.pdf</code> and <code>Book.pdf.mark</code>). From the Files app, select both files together.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I read offline?</summary>
<p>Documents you open are kept on your device. To save your whole library, tap <strong>Sync All</strong> on the start screen. In <strong>Settings</strong> you can sync automatically when the app opens, on Wi-Fi only by default.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why doesn't search find a word?</summary>
<p>Handwriting is recognized on your device after a document opens, which takes a moment for long notebooks; a banner shows the progress. Recognition works best on clear handwriting and may miss very small or stylized writing.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I free up space or remove my data?</summary>
<p><strong>Settings → Remove Downloaded Documents</strong> deletes saved copies. <strong>Settings → Disconnect Dropbox</strong> also removes the sign-in, recents and favorites. Your Dropbox is not affected. See the <a href="/sumi/privacy/">privacy policy</a> for details.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there a Mac version?</summary>
<p>Yes. The free Mac app opens notebooks with a double-click and adds Quick Look previews in Finder. Download it from <a href="https://github.com/schaefer-dev/supernote-viewer">GitHub</a>.</p>
</details>

</div>
