# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Sumi for Supernote on iPhone and iPad (the "app") displays Supernote notebooks (`.note`) and annotated PDFs (`.mark` with their original PDF) on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: **the developer collects no data at all.**

## Who is responsible

Daniel Schäfer, Germany. Contact: [sumi@schaefer-dev.de](mailto:sumi@schaefer-dev.de)

## What the app does not do

- It has no user accounts, servers or backend of its own.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- It does not send your documents, account information or usage data to the developer or to any third party.

## Dropbox

If you choose to connect Dropbox, you sign in on Dropbox's own page and grant the app **read-only** access. The app then communicates directly between your device and Dropbox to:

- read your account name and identifier, shown in Settings;
- list folders and file details (names, sizes, modification dates, revisions);
- download the documents you open or sync.

The app never changes, uploads or deletes anything in your Dropbox. The sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device by the official Dropbox SDK. Your use of Dropbox is governed by [Dropbox's privacy policy](https://www.dropbox.com/privacy).

## Data stored on your device

To work offline and reopen documents quickly, the app keeps on your device only:

- rendered, searchable PDFs of documents you opened or synced;
- cached handwriting-recognition results;
- your recent documents, favorites, reading positions, chosen library folder and the connected Dropbox account's name and identifier.

Saved documents are excluded from iCloud backup. Handwriting recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework; your handwriting never leaves the device for recognition.

You can remove downloaded documents at any time in **Settings → Remove Downloaded Documents**. **Disconnect Dropbox** removes the account's sign-in, recents, favorites, library folder and downloaded documents from the device. Deleting the app removes all of its data. None of these actions affect the files in your Dropbox.

## Files you open without Dropbox

Documents you open from the Files app, from another app or from the bundled samples are copied into a temporary area for viewing and are not kept after the session.

## Contacting support

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to answer you and are not shared.
